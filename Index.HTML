Snakes are remarkably adapted reptiles that have survived on Earth for over 100 million years,
inhabiting every single continent except Antarctica. As ectothermic or "solar-powered" creatures,
they rely on the environment around them to regulate their body temperature, which makes them highly
efficient energy savers. Without arms or legs, they move with incredible grace using specialized scales
and distinct slithering techniques like sidewinding or concertina movement to navigate through deserts,
oceans, and high tree branches.
